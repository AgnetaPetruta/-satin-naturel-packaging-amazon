The challenge

SATIN NATUREL came to Fluidspace for further visual design support across digital and print. With a refreshed identity already in development, their in-house team needed help bringing that direction into everyday communication.

The ambition

Translate the refreshed identity into consistent packaging, Amazon banners, and product visuals, making each product distinctive, recognizable, and easy to understand.

Supporting a Brand in Transition

Working alongside SATIN NATUREL’s in-house designers, we helped bring the refreshed identity into packaging, Amazon banners, and product visuals.

Through visual proposals and ongoing feedback, we developed applications that stayed connected to the new direction while giving individual products their own character.

01 / PackagingExploration / Ingredients / Texture

A Fresh Look at the Ingredients

Together with Stefan and SATIN NATUREL’s in-house design team, we explored different packaging directions through brainstorming, visual proposals, and ongoing feedback.

We showcased the ingredients on the packaging to bring more credibility and make the connection to the product clearer. Natural textures gave each design its own character while keeping the range connected to the refreshed identity.

02 / Amazon BannersIngredients / Product Visuals

Amazon Banners

For the Amazon banners, SATIN NATUREL wanted to retain the darker, more premium aesthetic of its product communication.

We worked with rich backgrounds, carefully arranged ingredients, and clear product compositions to make the supplements feel both refined and approachable.

Showing the ingredients alongside the bottles was particularly important. It gave customers a more immediate understanding of what they were looking at, while keeping the product itself at the center of the composition.

A Closer Look at What’s Inside

We continued exploring how product visuals could communicate more than appearance.

By showing the capsules alongside the packaging, we made the product’s format immediately visible. Combined with the ingredient photography, this created a more informative presentation without compromising the premium look.

The goal was simple: help customers understand the product at a glance.

Consistency Across the Range

As the supplement range expanded, maintaining a recognizable visual language became increasingly important.

For products such as Burning Collagen, we continued using the same approach: strong product visibility, carefully selected ingredients, and a consistent visual atmosphere.

This allowed individual products to stand out while still feeling part of the same family.

A Collaboration That Grew with the Brand

Working with SATIN NATUREL was a genuinely rewarding collaboration.

From packaging explorations to Amazon product visuals, we enjoyed being part of the brand’s evolution and helping its growing wellness and supplement range find a consistent visual expression.

It was a pleasure working alongside their team and bringing these ideas to life.
